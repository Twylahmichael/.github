# User guide

Plain steps for the people who use the system every day. Everything works on a phone.

Sign in at `your-website/login` (or tap **Staff login** at the bottom of the site). The first time a new staff member signs in they are asked to choose their own password.

---

## Owner

You see everything for all your BNBs.

### Check how the day is going
1. Tap **Dashboard**. You see who arrives and leaves today, how many guests are in the house, occupancy, this month's income, and money still owed.
2. A red box **Payments needing review** means something needs your eyes (see "What to do if M-Pesa fails").
3. Use the property menu at the top to look at one BNB or **All properties**.

### Add or edit a property, unit or price
1. **Properties** → pick a property (or **Add property**).
2. **Details** tab: name, address, phone, WhatsApp number, check-in/out times, house rules. Tap **Save changes**.
3. **Units & rates** tab → **Edit unit & rates**: nightly rate, weekend rate (Friday and Saturday nights), cleaning fee (charged once per stay), minimum nights.
4. For Christmas or other busy seasons scroll to **Seasonal & holiday rates**, enter the dates and the rate, and tap **Add seasonal rate**. It replaces the normal price on those nights.
5. **Photos** tab: tap **Add photos**. Use the star for the cover photo and the arrows to reorder.
6. To stop a unit being booked online untick **Available for booking**. Units with bookings are hidden, never deleted.

### Add staff
1. **Staff** → **Add staff**.
2. Enter their name, email, a temporary password, the role and which properties they can work in. Give them the password privately.
3. To stop someone's access tap **Deactivate**. They are signed out and blocked straight away.

### See profit
1. **Finance** → **Monthly profit**. Pick the month with the arrows. You see income, expenses and profit for each BNB and combined.
2. **Expenses** tab → **Add expense**: property, category, amount, date, a photo of the receipt. Mistakes: edit and tick **Void** (managers cannot delete; you can).
3. **Reports** shows occupancy, average nightly rate, revenue by BNB and by source, top guests and cancellations. **Export CSV** opens in Excel.

### Settings
**Settings** → business name and logo, support WhatsApp number, deposit percentage, how long dates are held, cancellation policy, reminder timings, and the Paybill/Till number shown to guests who pay manually. **Audit log** shows who changed what.

## Manager

You manage the properties the owner gave you.

### Take a booking over the phone or at the door
1. **Bookings** → **New booking** (or tap free nights on the **Calendar**).
2. Choose the dates. Only units that are free show up.
3. Type the guest's phone number. Returning guests are found automatically. Add the name.
4. Check the price. Add a discount or a deposit if needed. If they paid something now enter it under **Payment received now**.
5. Tap **Create booking**. It is confirmed straight away.

### Check a guest in and out
1. Open the booking (Dashboard → Arriving today, or the Calendar).
2. Tap **Check in**. If money is still owed you get a reminder, but you can continue.
3. Collect any balance: **Request payment by M-Pesa** (the guest gets a prompt on their phone) or **Record manual payment** (cash, bank, or an M-Pesa code you were sent).
4. On the day they leave tap **Check out**. A cleaning task appears automatically.

### Block dates (maintenance or personal use)
1. **Calendar**. Drag across the free nights on the right unit's row (on a phone: tap the first night, then the last night).
2. Choose **Block these dates**, add a reason.
3. To open them again tap the grey bar → **Unblock dates**.

### Cancel a booking
Open it → **Cancel** → write the reason. The dates are free again immediately. The system never refunds by itself: pay the refund yourself, then use **Record manual payment** → type **Refund** so the books are right.

### Housekeeping
**Housekeeping** shows the tasks for your properties. Pick a cleaner in each task, and tap **Mark inspected** after a unit has been cleaned. **Print checklist** gives a paper list.

### Messages
Each booking has a **WhatsApp messages** box. If automatic sending is off or fails, tap **Send via WhatsApp** and the message opens ready to send from your own phone.

## Cleaner

You only see **Housekeeping**.

1. Open **Housekeeping**. Units that need cleaning are listed under their property. A red label means **a guest arrives today: do this one first**.
2. Tap **Start cleaning** when you begin.
3. Add a note if something is broken or missing (for example "lamp broken").
4. Tap **Mark clean** when finished. The manager will inspect it.
5. Made a mistake? Tap **Undo**.

---

## How guests book and pay

1. They search dates on the website, pick a place, enter name and phone, and tick the privacy box.
2. They tap **Pay deposit with M-Pesa**, see the prompt on their phone, and enter their PIN.
3. The booking confirms by itself. They get a WhatsApp message with a link to their booking page, receipts and your contact details. They pay the balance from the same page.
4. Dates are held for a few minutes while they pay. If the deposit does not arrive the dates are released.

Guests can also tap **Book on WhatsApp**: it opens WhatsApp with their dates and price already typed, and you reply.

## What to do if M-Pesa fails

- **The guest says they did not get the prompt.** Ask them to check they used the number registered on M-Pesa and that the phone has signal, then tap **Try again** (guest page) or **Request payment by M-Pesa** (staff). You can only send 3 prompts per 10 minutes.
- **The guest paid but the booking still says pending.** Wait two minutes: the system checks with Safaricom automatically. If still pending, ask for the M-Pesa message and use **Record manual payment** → *M-Pesa (manual)* and type the code (for example `SHK4T7XYZ1`). Each code can be used once.
- **Dashboard says "Payment needs review".** Open the booking and read the note. Usual causes: the guest paid after the dates were released to someone else (refund them or move them), or the amount received was different from the amount asked (check the M-Pesa statement and record the right amount). Tap **Mark as reviewed** when done (owner).
- **Paying by Paybill/Till instead.** The guest page shows the number and the booking reference to use as the account. Record the payment manually once you see it.
- **The page says M-Pesa is not reachable.** Try again in a minute, or take the payment manually. Bookings are never lost: nothing is confirmed until money is recorded.

## How to block dates
See "Block dates" under Manager above. Blocked dates cannot be booked from the website or by staff until you unblock them.

## Good to know
- All times are Nairobi time; all amounts are whole Kenya shillings.
- The moon icon switches between light and dark.
- Forgot a password? The owner can set a new temporary one in **Staff**.
